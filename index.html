Common questions

straight answers
What file formats do you accept?

Anything with audio. MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG, M4A. Streaming links work too — SoundCloud, YouTube, Bandcamp, Dropbox, WeTransfer. Studio quality preferred, but we work with what you've got.

What do I actually get?

One single HTML file. Maybe 200-300 lines of code. Three.js scene rendered in your browser, beams synced to your audio. Double-click it, opens in any modern browser. Embed it via iframe on your website, label page, Bandcamp, NFT marketplace. No server required. No subscription. No platform lock-in. It is yours forever.

What are the 5 brain layers?

Prediction error · pattern break · emotional hook · tension · release. These are real neurological responses to music — not made up. Each one becomes a beam of light with its own colour, behaviour, and timing. The beams charge during verses and fire at transitions. Watching the visualisation is watching your song's narrative made visible.

How long does it take?

Single track: 24 hours. Album pack: 5 days. Custom: depends on scope, we'll quote you. Revisions are unlimited and free — we want you to love it.

Can I use this commercially?

Yes. The file is yours. Use it on your website, in promotional material, on streaming services that allow video, in music videos, in NFT drops, anywhere. No royalties owed to us.

Who is behind this?

AudioFabric is built by Thomas Frumkin (architect of the visualisation engine) and Simon Gant (delivery, sales, customer relationships). Two-person operation. No middlemen. You can reach either of us directly.

Refund policy?

If the final visualisation doesn't capture your song, we'll keep iterating until it does. If we genuinely can't make it work, full refund. No questions.
